# Pop-Mart-Multi-Channel-Profitability-Analysis
Case Study: I analysed 2 years of retail data to assess the performance of Pop Marts' Store, Online and RoboShop channel. Sales, operating profit, profit margins, running-cost efficiency, year-on-year growth, budget performance and results were compared across countries. The goal was to find out what the company's best revenue drivers were, what channels were the most efficient and what potential for investment had the greatest potential to grow.
